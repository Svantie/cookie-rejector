# Take control of your privacy without the hassle of cookie banners.

### This extension automatically handles GDPR consent prompts by intercepting the Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) API. Instead of manually clicking through complex settings on every website, the extension automatically sets your preferences to maximize privacy.

Key Features:

- Auto-Reject Cookies: Automatically denies non-essential tracking and advertising cookies.

- Disable Legitimate Interest: Automatically opts out of "Legitimate Interest" tracking, preventing vendors from bypassing your consent.

- TCF API Interception: Handles consent directly at the API level for faster browsing and fewer disruptive pop-ups.

- Lightweight & Privacy-Focused: Runs smoothly in the background without tracking your browsing activity.
